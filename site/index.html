One shared folder for your Windows and Mac computers, open from your phone too.

It syncs over your own network, not the internet.

Download for Windows Download for Mac (Apple silicon)

Free and open source. Windows 10 and later, macOS 13 and later on Apple silicon.

One folder

You get one folder on each computer, and the app keeps them the same. Files stay ordinary files on your disk, so any other program can use them.

  1. Install Satchel on each computer.
  2. Pair them once. Each shows the other's device ID, you confirm it, and they find each other on the network from then on.
  3. Use the folder. From Satchel, from Explorer or Finder, from any app.

Your network, not the internet

Changes go straight between your computers. There is no account, no relay and no telemetry. The bundled Syncthing engine encrypts every transfer, and a computer you do not fully trust can hold an encrypted copy it cannot read.

Notes and a pinboard

A note editor with history, a shared pinboard, and search over everything in the folder. Notes are Markdown files; nothing is locked into the app.

Quick drop and the drawer

A thin handle sits at the screen edge. Drag a file toward it and the drawer slides out; drop, and it closes by itself. Paste text or a screenshot, or drag something back out into another app. A hotkey, Ctrl or ⌘ Shift Space, opens it anywhere.

Your phone

Scan a code, and the phone opens the folder in the browser over your network. Read and edit notes, download files when you need them, upload photos. Nothing is stored on the phone. Add it to the Home Screen and it opens like an app.

Open source

Satchel is MIT licensed and developed in the open on GitHub. Installers for Windows and Apple silicon Macs come with published checksums, so you can check what you downloaded. The installers are not code-signed, so each system asks once. On Windows choose More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, open the app once so it is refused, then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway. Updates after that come through the app and are checked against their checksums.
